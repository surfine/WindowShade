# WindowShade 1.0.19 · 手感与稳定

[产品官网](https://windowshade.aaronlau.me/) · [English website](https://windowshade.aaronlau.me/en/)

已发布（2026-11-01）。

## 中文

### 一句话摘要（appcast 用）

刘海照系统灵动岛重做：停上去不再展开，长按或两指张开展开；上推一下把整屏窗口收进刘海，还能整批撤销；左右半屏和甩一下先由一层代理跟手；整理菜单多一步撤销；收起动画默认开。需要 macOS 15 或更新版本。

### 升级前请看这里

- **从 1.0.18 升级照旧**：菜单里「检查更新」一键安装，授权不用重开。
- **悬停不再展开那一排。** 指针停在刘海上不再自动展开；改成长按刘海、两指张开，或键盘聚焦时按空格。这是有意的改动。
- **收起窗口的动画这一版起默认开。** 没动过这个开关的人也会看到动画；不想要就在 设置 → 效果 关掉。以前明确关过的人仍然关着。
- **「提示音」默认开。** 一个开关管等你、完成、番茄钟、计时器到点、指挥拍子。原来只有计时器会响；这一版起番茄钟休息和指挥拍子也会响，不需要就在 设置 → 刘海 关掉。
- **`windowshade://lock` 链接移除了。** 旧的快捷指令会失效；改用「锁屏并收起私人 App」快捷指令动作（先在 高级 → 实验 开「离开就锁」并选好设备）。
- **需要 macOS 15 或更新版本**（Apple Silicon）。
- **第一次下载的人拿到的是 DMG 安装包**：把 WindowShade 拖进「应用程序」就好；更新还是走菜单里的「检查更新」。

### 新增

- **刘海照系统灵动岛重做。** 紧凑两耳、最小一颗圆、提醒和那一排同一套尺寸；那一排固定宽高、左右滚动，右缘淡出；点耳朵打开那件活动，点刘海本体回主屏幕；长按有东西就展开，两指张开展开那一排。
- **上推全收。** 两指在刘海上往上推够远、够果断，整块屏的窗口一起收进刘海；推的过程跟手，过线提示「松手全部收进刘海」，收完给「已收起 N 扇 · 撤销」，一下撤销整批。
- **拖进刘海要停一下。** 拖窗口或文件到刘海，落点停 0.15 秒才亮；划过去不算。
- **左右半屏和甩一下，先动代理。** 一层代理跟着手指走，松手才写一次真窗口；飞到一半可以用两指接住改道。有屏幕录制用窗口截图，没有就用描边。
- **整理 ▸ 撤销〈最近一步〉。** 收起、收进刘海、侧拉、画中画、排布都能按顺序撤销；只记这一次运行，最多 20 步。
- **双击标题栏可以照系统设置。** 设置 → 手势 里选「收起窗口」或「照系统设置」。
- **一个「提示音」开关。** 等你、完成、番茄钟、计时器、指挥拍子共用一个；卷帘收起 / 展开的效果声仍单独一行。
- **隐私页多了「摄像头是不是正被用着」。** 只读系统状态，不申请摄像头权限。

### 改进与修复

- **防误触。** 长按统一 0.5 秒；同一扇窗 0.6 秒内任何方向的手势都挡，往回撤销放行；审批卡刚出现 0.5 秒内的按下不算；晃一晃只认左右。
- **收起动画更快、有兜底。** 0.25 秒落定；没有屏幕录制时用描边；减少动态效果时原处淡出、新处淡入。
- **慢 App 上的双击不漏。** 标题栏双击改到后台判定，主线程卡 600 毫秒也仍然收起，不会漏给系统去缩放或最小化。
- **刘海里的提醒不再互相顶掉。** 提醒、日程、计时器、番茄钟各有各的位置，一个说完另一个原样回来。
- **多屏。** 实时活动跟着正在用的那块屏；收进刘海的窗口每块屏都显示。
- **视频通话时刘海不抢镜。** 摄像头在用时，提醒只留一个点，不把岛撑大。
- **DockDoor 等同类工具提示一次。** 标题栏手势被别的工具抢走时，刘海说一次。
- 编程会话、审批卡、指挥页仍默认关、藏在预览旗标后面；这一版只修了它们的稳定性，没有对外打开。

### 变更与移除

- 悬停不再展开那一排（见上）。
- 收起动画默认开（见上）。
- 提示音默认开，番茄钟休息与指挥拍子加入（见上）。
- `windowshade://lock` 移除，改用快捷指令动作（见上）。

### 仍待真机

- 那一排 408 × 160 的横滑手感、耳朵点击、长按展开。尺寸先用暂定值，11 月 6 日真机量完在下一版微调。
- 多屏跟随、FaceTime 摄像头占用、DockDoor 共存。
- 刷脸、注视、声纹、代填仍默认关；没标定不会执行，攻击矩阵还没跑。

## English

### One-line summary (for the appcast)

The notch now follows the system's Dynamic Island: hover no longer expands it — long-press or pinch out instead; push up to tuck every window and undo the whole batch; half-screen and throw gestures move a proxy first; the Arrange menu gains Undo; the fold animation is on by default. Requires macOS 15 or later.

### Before you update

- **From 1.0.18: one click**, as always. Permissions stay on.
- **Hover no longer expands the row.** Resting the pointer on the notch does nothing; long-press it, pinch out, or press Space when the island has keyboard focus. This is intentional.
- **The fold animation is on by default.** Anyone who never touched this switch will see it; turn it off in Settings → Effects. An explicit off stays off.
- **One "Alert sound" switch is on by default.** It covers waiting, done, Pomodoro, timer and conductor beats. Only the timer used to sound; Pomodoro breaks and conductor beats now do too. Turn it off in Settings → Notch.
- **The `windowshade://lock` link is gone.** Old shortcuts stop working; use the "Lock screen and tuck private apps" Shortcuts action instead (enable Presence Lock under Advanced → Experiments and pick a device first).
- **Requires macOS 15 or later** (Apple Silicon).
- **First downloads come as a DMG**: drag WindowShade into Applications. Updates still go through Check for Updates.

### New

- **The notch is rebuilt around the system's Dynamic Island.** Compact ears, a single minimal circle, alerts and the row share one set of sizes; the row has a fixed size and scrolls sideways with a trailing fade; click an ear to open that activity, click the notch body to go home; long-press when there is anything inside, or pinch out.
- **Push up to tuck everything.** A deliberate two-finger push on the notch tucks every window on that screen, follows your fingers, offers "Release to tuck everything", and ends with "N tucked · Undo" that undoes the whole batch.
- **Dropping onto the notch requires a dwell.** The landing target arms after 0.15 s; a fast sweep across does not.
- **Half-screen and throw gestures move a proxy first.** A translucent proxy follows your fingers and the real window is written once on release; catch it mid-flight with two fingers to re-aim. Uses a window snapshot when Screen Recording is available, an outline otherwise.
- **Arrange ▸ Undo the last step.** Shade, tuck, slide-over, PiP and placement undo in order; memory only, up to 20 steps this run.
- **Double-click the title bar can follow the system.** Settings → Gestures offers "Tuck the window" or "Follow the system setting".
- **One "Alert sound" switch** for waiting, done, Pomodoro, timer and conductor beats; the fold/unfold effect sound stays on its own row.
- **The privacy page lists whether a camera is in use.** Read-only system state; no camera permission is requested.

### Improvements and fixes

- **Fewer accidental gestures.** Long-press is 0.5 s everywhere; any direction is blocked for 0.6 s after a committed gesture (the reverse still passes); a press within 0.5 s of an approval card appearing is ignored; shake only counts horizontally.
- **The fold animation is faster and has a fallback.** Settles in 0.25 s; an outline when Screen Recording is unavailable; cross-fade in place under Reduce Motion.
- **Double-click no longer leaks on a busy app.** The decision runs off the main thread, so a 600 ms stall still tucks instead of zooming or minimizing.
- **Notch reminders no longer displace each other.** Alerts, calendar, timer and Pomodoro have separate seats and come back unchanged.
- **Multiple displays.** Live activities follow the display you are using; tucked windows still appear on every display.
- **The notch stays quiet during video calls.** Alerts collapse to a dot instead of growing the island.
- **A one-time hint when another tool owns title-bar gestures**, such as DockDoor.
- Coding sessions, approval cards and the conductor page remain off by default behind the preview flag; this release only makes them steadier.

### Changed and removed

- Hover no longer expands the row (see above).
- The fold animation is on by default (see above).
- Alert sound is on by default, and now includes Pomodoro breaks and conductor beats (see above).
- `windowshade://lock` is removed in favour of the Shortcuts action (see above).

### Still needs real hardware

- The 408 × 160 row's scroll feel, ear clicks and long-press expansion. Sizes are provisional; they will be tuned in the next version after measuring on 11 November.
- Multi-display following, FaceTime camera-in-use and DockDoor coexistence.
- Face recognition, gaze, voiceprint and autofill remain off by default; nothing runs until calibrated, and the attack matrix has not been run.
