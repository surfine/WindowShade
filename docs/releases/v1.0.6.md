# WindowShade v1.0.6

## 中文

### 这次更新

置顶预览与性能升级。

- 相同窗口内容使用缓存，减少重复截图
- 预览面板跟随源窗口的 Space
- 增加挂起/恢复全部置顶预览的操作，会话不会丢失

## English

### Highlights

Pinned previews and performance improvements.

- Cache repeated captures of the same window content
- Preview panels follow their source window across Spaces
- Suspend or resume all pinned previews without losing the session
