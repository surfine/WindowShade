# WindowShade v1.0.2

## 中文

### 这次更新

置顶预览更聪明。

- 预览会跟随源窗口跨桌面（Space）移动
- 切换桌面动画期间暂停追踪，避免闪烁
- 折叠置顶窗口前先停止预览
- 标题栏双击不会误触地址栏和按钮
- 重叠预览会自动让开

权限和快捷键保持不变。

## English

### Highlights

Pinned previews now follow the source window more reliably.

- Previews follow their source across Spaces
- Tracking pauses during Space transitions
- A pinned preview stops before its source is folded
- Title-bar double-click avoids address fields and buttons
- Overlapping previews yield to the one being used

Permissions and shortcuts are unchanged.
