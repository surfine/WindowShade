# WindowShade 1.0.16 · 像 iPad 一样多任务

[产品官网](https://windowshade.aaronlau.me/) · [窗口往事](https://windowshade.aaronlau.me/history/) · [English website](https://windowshade.aaronlau.me/en/)

收起窗口是为了让开。这一版给“让开”多了几个去处：收进刘海、靠在屏幕边（侧拉）、缩到角落（画中画）；也把排窗补齐到从 Rectangle、Raycast 换过来不用改手势和快捷键。第一次打开有三步，说清楚它做什么、你之前常用哪个、再打开两项授权。

## 中文

### 欢迎使用 WindowShade

第一次打开时，三步：先说自己做什么，再问你之前常用哪个系统，最后打开辅助功能和屏幕录制两项授权。菜单里的“欢迎使用 WindowShade…”随时能再打开；打开“减少动态效果”时不动。

### 刘海

- **收进刘海。** 朝刘海甩一下标题栏，或拖到刘海下面垂下的小岛上，窗口就收进刘海；指针停在刘海上展开一排，点一下放回原处，停在某一格上能看一眼它此刻的样子。
- **刘海是主屏幕键。** 点一下回到主屏幕，点两下是调度中心，点三下是这个 App 的所有窗口。
- **一排收着所有让开的窗口。** 收进刘海的、侧拉的、卷帘条、带到每张桌面的，以及系统里最小化的窗口、隐藏的 App，都在这一排里。
- **两指往上推，这块屏上的窗口全部收进刘海；往下拉，整批回来。** 左右滑切到上一个、下一个 App。
- **拖着标题栏到刘海，小岛上有五格：** 左半屏、铺满屏幕、收进刘海、魔法平铺、右半屏。
- **每一下都有回应。** 做成了、做不到、为什么做不到，都在刘海上说一声；第一次遇到某个手势时，刘海里放一段小动画教一下，同一条最多三次，点一下就不再出。
- 把文件拖到刘海上停一下，主屏幕只亮能打开它的 App。外接显示器顶部正中也有一段看不见的“刘海”。

### 像 iPad 一样多任务

- **侧拉。** 拖着标题栏到屏幕左右边的中段停一下，窗口靠在边上、浮在前面，外面一圈玻璃边框；拖角上那段弧改大小，拖玻璃边框挪位置，往屏幕边一甩就收起，边上留一小片箭头，点一下或往里拉就回来。每张桌面上都有。想要快捷键，在设置 → 快捷键里自己录一个。
- **分屏。** 两扇窗口拼满一块屏时，中间出现一根小竖条：拖它两扇一起变，松手吸到三分之一、一半、三分之二；推到屏幕边，那一扇进侧拉。四角、三分、网格排好的窗口，每条缝都能这样拖。
- **画中画。** 拖着标题栏到屏幕角落停一下，窗口缩成一张实时画面浮在角落，原窗口让开。甩到别的角、推出屏幕边藏起来、三档大小；滚轮和“上一页 / 下一页”直接滚原窗口；可以只看窗口里的一块；双击回到原处。任意窗口都行，也可以同时开几个。
- **启动台。** macOS 26 起系统没有了启动台，这里补回来：主屏幕、文件夹、App 资料库、打字就搜（拼音、首字母都行）。按住图标拖出来，直接放到半屏、铺满或侧拉。

### 排窗

- **魔法平铺。** 在标题栏上两指张开，这块屏上的窗口一次排好：浏览器、写代码的当主角，写作、笔记作参考，聊天放侧拉；比例在 5:5、6:4、7:3 里自己定，竖着放的屏幕上下分。捏合整批撤回。想要快捷键，在设置 → 快捷键里自己录一个。
- **卷轴。** 放不下的窗口一列挨一列接在屏幕右边外，只露一条边；在标题栏上两指左右滑，整条跟着手指走，松手带着惯性滑到最近的列（niri 的做法）。指针停在露出来的那一条上，看一眼那扇窗此刻的样子；在卷轴里的标题栏上两指张开是卷轴概览：整条缩小铺开，点哪扇就滑到哪一列。
- **晃一晃。** 拖着标题栏来回晃，别的窗口收进刘海；再晃一下放回来（Windows 的 Aero Shake）。
- **网格。** 上半或下半一行里左右推，得到三分之二和六分之一；再拐一下是九分之一。
- **从 Rectangle、Raycast 换过来。** 设置 → 快捷键 → 更多排法，点“换上”，就用回 Rectangle 那一套快捷键（装过的照它现在的设置）；多了上半屏、下半屏、居中、大一点、小一点、高度占满、移到上一块屏幕。Raycast 的窗口命令名一一对照。
- **从 Swish 换过来。** 设置 → 快捷键 → 更多排法 → 方向键换成字母：WASD、IJKL 或 HJKL，⌃⌥ 加字母；点“方向键”换回。
- **移到另一块屏幕（⌃⌘N）**，上下摆的显示器也行；**窗口之间留缝**（不留 / 窄 / 宽）。
- **做不到时说一声。** 改不了大小的窗口放在目标区域正中，浮窗说清楚。

### 找窗口、切窗口

- **按窗口切换。** 按住 ⌥ 连按 Tab，一扇一扇窗口地挑，收着的也在里面；数字键直接跳，W 关、Q 退。设置里可以换成 ⌘Tab。
- **调度中心里 ⌘W 关掉指针指着的那扇，⌘Q 退出它的 App。**
- **Dock。** 再点一下最前面那个 App 的 Dock 图标，它让开；Dock 可以留在一块屏上，不跟着指针跑。窗口浏览的预览里多了进入全屏、新建窗口（Mac 上是 ⌘N）、让开这个 App、退出 App；关掉的窗口，卡片缩小淡出。
- **把预览拖出去排。** 把窗口浏览里的一张卡片拖出面板，贴着屏幕左边、右边或顶边松手，那扇窗就左半屏、右半屏或铺满屏幕；拖到屏幕边中段停一下是侧拉。
- **小程序的窗口找得到了。** 微信小程序的窗口列在微信名下。
- **Dock 图标上两指上下滑**（设置 → 窗口浏览里打开，默认关）：往上看这个 App 的所有窗口，往下让开这个 App。
- **暂时取消全部置顶。** 一下让开所有置顶的窗口，再点一下按原来的前后顺序放回。

### 更省电

- 桌面倾斜效果的传感器读数从每秒约 800 次降到 62 次（桌面效果本来每秒只画 60 帧），开着效果时常驻 CPU 从约 5% 降到 2–3%。
- 盒盖和倾斜效果改成默认关，设置里还能打开。

## English

### Welcome to WindowShade

The first time you open it, three steps: what it does, which system you came from, and the two permissions. Open it again any time from Welcome to WindowShade… in the menu; with Reduce Motion on, it stays still.

### The notch

- **Tuck windows into the notch.** Flick a title bar at the notch, or drop it on the island that hangs below it. Rest the pointer on the notch to see them in a row; click one to put it back, or rest on it to glance at it as it is now.
- **The notch is a Home button.** One click goes to the Home screen, two open Mission Control, three show every window of the current app.
- **One row for everything that stepped aside:** tucked, in Slide Over, rolled up, carried to every desktop, plus windows the system minimized and apps you hid.
- **Push up with two fingers to tuck every window on that screen; pull down to bring the whole batch back.** Swipe left or right to switch apps.
- **Drag a title bar to the notch and the island offers five places:** left half, fill, the notch, Magic Tiling, right half.
- **Every action answers.** What happened, what couldn't and why appear on the notch; the first time a gesture would help, a short animation in the notch shows it — at most three times, and a click dismisses it for good.
- Drop a file on the notch and the Home screen lights only the apps that can open it. External displays get an invisible "notch" at the top centre.

### Multitasking like an iPad

- **Slide Over.** Hold a title bar over the middle of the left or right screen edge: the window rests at the edge above everything, with a glass border. Drag the arc in its corner to resize, drag the border to move it, flick it off the edge to tuck it away, and bring it back from the small arrow left there. It's on every desktop. Record a shortcut for it in Settings → Shortcuts if you want one.
- **Split View.** When two windows fill a screen side by side, a small bar appears between them: drag it to resize both, let go to snap to a third, a half or two thirds, or push it to the edge to send that window into Slide Over. Every seam between tiled windows — corners, thirds, grids — works the same way.
- **Picture in Picture.** Hold a title bar in a screen corner and the window shrinks to a live picture there while the real one steps aside. Fling it to another corner or off the edge, pick one of three sizes, scroll or page the real window from it, show just one region, and double-click to put it back. Any window, several at once.
- **Launchpad.** macOS 26 took it away; it's back: Home screen, folders, App Library, type to search. Drag an icon straight into half the screen, full screen or Slide Over.

### Arranging windows

- **Magic Tiling.** Spread two fingers on a title bar and the screen is arranged at once: browsers and code editors lead, writing and notes sit beside them, chat goes to Slide Over; 5:5, 6:4 or 7:3 is chosen for you, and a portrait screen splits top and bottom. Pinch to undo it all. Record a shortcut for it in Settings → Shortcuts if you want one.
- **Scroll strip.** Windows that don't fit wait in columns past the right edge with a sliver showing; swipe two fingers on a title bar to slide the whole strip with your fingers, and it carries on with its momentum to the nearest column (the niri way). Rest the pointer on the sliver to glance at that window as it is now; spread two fingers on a strip window's title bar for the strip overview, which lays the whole strip out small — click a window to slide to its column.
- **Shake.** Shake a window by its title bar and the others go into the notch; shake again to bring them back (Windows' Aero Shake).
- **Grid.** Within the top or bottom row, pushing sideways gives two-thirds and sixths; one more turn gives ninths.
- **Coming from Rectangle or Raycast.** Settings → Shortcuts → More layouts → Use Rectangle's shortcuts brings its key set with you (your current Rectangle settings if you have it). New: top and bottom half, centre, larger, smaller, full height and previous display. Raycast's window commands map one to one.
- **Coming from Swish.** Settings → Shortcuts → More layouts → letters for the arrow keys: WASD, IJKL or HJKL with ⌃⌥; pick Arrows to go back.
- **Move to another display (⌃⌘N)**, including displays above or below; **gaps between windows** (none, narrow, wide).
- **Says so when it can't.** A window that can't be resized is centred in its target and the bubble tells you why.

### Finding and switching windows

- **Switch by window.** Hold ⌥ and press Tab to pick window by window, rolled-up ones included; number keys jump, W closes, Q quits. ⌘Tab is an option in Settings.
- **In Mission Control, ⌘W closes the window under the pointer and ⌘Q quits its app.**
- **The Dock.** Clicking the icon of the app in front puts it out of the way; the Dock can stay on one display. The window browser's previews add Enter Full Screen, New Window (⌘N on a Mac), Hide App and Quit App; a closed window's card shrinks away.
- **Drag a preview out to place it.** Drag a card out of the window browser and let go against the left, right or top edge of the screen for left half, right half or fill; hold it over the middle of a side edge for Slide Over.
- **Mini-program windows turn up.** WeChat mini-program windows are listed under WeChat.
- **Two-finger swipes on Dock icons** (off by default; turn on in Settings → Window browser): up shows all of that app's windows, down hides the app.
- **Temporarily unpin all** gets every pinned window out of the way; again puts them back in the same order.

### Lighter

- The tilt effect reads its sensor about 62 times a second instead of about 800 (the desktop effect only draws 60 frames a second); with the effect on, idle CPU drops from about 5% to 2–3%.
- The lid and tilt effects are now off by default; turn them on in Settings.
