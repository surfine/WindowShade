# WindowShade 1.0.15 · 不用去了再回来

[产品官网](https://windowshade.aaronlau.me/) · [窗口往事](https://windowshade.aaronlau.me/history/) · [English website](https://windowshade.aaronlau.me/en/) · [介绍视频](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1K7ag6WEdH/)

收起窗口是为了让开。这一版让你不必展开、不必切桌面，也能看一眼；还可以在标题栏上滑动、甩一下或用 ⌃⌘ 方向键，把窗口收起、铺满，或排成半屏、三分之一、四角。

## 中文

### 9 月 26 日更新（版本号不变，重新发布）

- **左右一架梯子。** 在标题栏上往左滑占左半屏；朝同一边再滑，变成左三分之二、左三分之一；那边还有一块屏幕的话，再滑就移过去，落在靠近的那一半；没有就回到半屏。右边一样。
- **拐进角落。** 左右滑到位后接着往下或往上拐，窗口占那个角。手指只是走了弧线不会拐。
- **甩一下标题栏。** 拖着标题栏快速甩出去再松手，往哪边甩就排到哪边：往上收起，往下铺满，左右走同一架梯子，斜着甩占一角。慢慢拖、贴着屏幕边松手，还是系统原来的拖动和平铺。参照 iPadOS 26 的甩窗口，但往上仍是收起，不是全屏。
- **键盘也一样。** `⌃⌘←`、`⌃⌘→` 走左右梯子，`⌃⌘↓` 变大一级，`⌃⌘↑` 变小一级；按完 `⌃⌘←` 马上按 `⌃⌘↓` 占左下角。刚用 `⌃⌘↑` 收起的窗口，10 秒内按 `⌃⌘↓` 放回来。可在设置 → 快捷键 → 排布当前窗口里改键或关掉。
- **一本账。** 手势、快捷键、窗口浏览排过的窗口，捏合都能撤销，换显示器后都照原样排回去。只有屏幕尺寸真的变了才重排，外接屏菜单栏时隐时现不会让窗口来回跳。
- **卷帘条上的快捷键。** 卷帘条在最前面时，`⌘W`、`⌘M` 关掉或最小化那扇窗，`⌘H` 隐藏背后的 App，`⌘N` 交给它新建窗口，`⌘Q` 先把窗口放下来再退出它。原来按 `⌘Q` 会退出 WindowShade 自己。
- **用力按卷帘条。** Force Touch 触控板上用力按卷帘条也能看一眼，松手收回。
- 展开窗口后马上用手势排布，窗口不再被弹回原位。

### 9 月 25 日更新（版本号不变，重新发布）

- **窗口跟着手指走。** 在标题栏上往上推，窗口跟着手指收起；在卷帘条上往下拉，窗口放下来。松手才算数，往回拉就退回原样，真窗口从头到尾没被碰过。
- **跟得更快。** 手指一落到标题栏就开始准备，从认出标题栏到窗口能跟手：收起约 140 毫秒，展开约 100 毫秒（机器忙时原来要 300–490 毫秒）。双击收起的动画也跟着快了。
- **换显示器后排回去。** 用手势铺满或占半屏的窗口，内外屏切换后按原样排到新屏幕上；人手改过尺寸的窗口不动，捏合撤销仍然可用。
- **Safari 标签不会误收起。** 在标签上横滑切换、手指往上飘时，不再被认成“上推收起”。
- **看一眼先显示已有画面**，不再额外等实时画面的第一帧；目标窗口不见了会马上清理。
- **带到每张桌面的卷帘条不再叠在一起**，放不下的收进“更多窗口”菜单。


### 看一眼

指针停在卷帘条上约 0.22 秒，下方出现一张预览卡片；移开，它自己收回。卷帘条留在原处，卡片与它隔开，四角沿用真窗口的圆角。单击卡片才真正展开窗口。

能拿到实时画面时，卡片持续更新；最小化等情况下显示截图，并标明“收起时的画面”。设置 → 卷帘 → 看一眼，默认打开；关掉后单击卷帘条恢复原来的小缩略图。

### 带到每张桌面

按 `⌃⌘G`，窗口留在自己的桌面，别的桌面右上角出现它的卷帘条。停在上面就能看一眼；单击画面或双击卷帘条回到那扇窗。再按一次快捷键，或点卷帘条上的叉，停止携带。窗口被隐藏或最小化时，卡片标明“不是实时画面”。

### 标题栏手势

指针停在标题栏上操作，提示浮窗会显示松手后的动作；往回拉可以取消。

| 动作 | 结果 |
| --- | --- |
| 两指往上推 | 收起窗口；已经铺满时先还原 |
| 两指往下拉 | 铺满菜单栏和 Dock 之间的屏幕 |
| 左右滑 | 左半屏／右半屏 |
| 两指张开／捏合 | 铺满／撤销上次手势排布 |
| 两指轻点两下 | 在铺满与原来大小之间切换 |
| 在卷帘条上往下拉或轻点两下 | 展开窗口 |

普通鼠标滚轮三格算一次。Magic Mouse 已加入单指滑动与轻点两下的适配，仍待真机验证。Safari 标签上的左右滑保留给 Safari；网页和列表内的滚动不接管。Swish 运行时，标题栏手势交给它。开关在设置 → 卷帘 → 标题栏手势，默认打开。

### 收起更快，也补好了收尾

收起及收起动画先用快速截图起步，实时画面随后接上。在同一台 Mac 上连续测试，不带动画时首次可见变化的中位数由 342ms 降到 147ms；带动画的热启动遮盖由 271ms 降到 199ms。原始测量条件与边界见 [性能记录](https://github.com/surfine/WindowShade/blob/v1.0.15/docs/performance.md)。

- 收起时指针恰好落在绿色按钮上，不再自动展开窗口去弹系统菜单。
- 看一眼期间主动切回源 App，不会把刚唤回的 App 再次隐藏。
- 关闭看一眼后立即移回卷帘条，重新准备已停止的实时画面。
- 携带窗口被最小化后，点击返回会先取消最小化。
- 中英文官网与 README 同步更新；官网的看一眼示意支持悬停、轻点和键盘。

## English

### September 26 update (same version, re-released)

- **One set of steps for left and right.** Swipe left on a title bar for the left half; keep swiping left for two thirds, then one third. If there's another display on that side, once more moves the window onto the nearer half of it; if not, it starts over at the half. The right side works the same way.
- **Turn into a corner.** Once a sideways swipe is complete, turn down or up and the window takes that corner. A merely curved swipe doesn't turn.
- **Flick the title bar.** Drag a title bar, throw it and let go, and the window goes where you threw it: up rolls it up, down fills the screen, left and right walk the same steps, a diagonal takes a corner. A slow drag, or letting go at the screen edge, is still the system's own dragging and tiling. It follows iPadOS 26's flick, except that up still rolls up rather than going full screen.
- **The keyboard does the same.** `⌃⌘←` and `⌃⌘→` walk the steps, `⌃⌘↓` goes one size up, `⌃⌘↑` one size down; `⌃⌘←` then `⌃⌘↓` right away takes the bottom-left corner. A window you just rolled up with `⌃⌘↑` comes back with `⌃⌘↓` within 10 seconds. Change or turn them off in Settings → Shortcuts.
- **One record.** Pinch undoes a placement from a gesture, a shortcut or the window browser, and all of them are laid out again after a display change. Windows only move when a screen's size really changes, so an external display's menu bar showing and hiding no longer shuffles them.
- **Shortcuts on a bar.** While a bar is in front, `⌘W` and `⌘M` close or minimize that window, `⌘H` hides the app behind it, `⌘N` asks it for a new window, and `⌘Q` puts the window back down before quitting it. Before, `⌘Q` quit WindowShade itself.
- **Press firmly on a bar.** On a Force Touch trackpad, pressing firmly on a bar glances at the window; let go and it goes away.
- Placing a window with a gesture right after unrolling it no longer snaps it back.

### September 25 update (same version, re-released)

- **The window follows your fingers.** Push up on a title bar and the window rolls up under them; pull down on a bar and it unrolls. Nothing happens until you let go, and pulling back leaves the real window untouched.
- **It keeps up.** Preparation starts as soon as your fingers land on a title bar: about 140 ms to follow a roll-up and 100 ms to follow an unroll (up to 300–490 ms before, on a busy machine). The double-click animation got faster too.
- **Windows go back after a display change.** Windows you filled or halved with a gesture are laid out the same way on the new screen; a window you resized by hand is left alone, and pinch-to-undo still works.
- **Safari tabs no longer roll up by accident** when a sideways tab swipe drifts upward.
- **Glance shows the picture it already has** instead of waiting for the first live frame, and cleans up at once if the window disappears.
- **Carried bars no longer pile up**; the ones that don't fit go into a “More Windows” menu.


### Glance without a round trip

Rest the pointer on a rolled-up bar for about 0.22 seconds and a card appears below it. Move away to dismiss it; click the card to unroll the window. The bar stays put, with a small gap separating it from the preview. Live pictures keep updating; minimized windows use a labelled snapshot. Glance is on by default in Settings → Roll up.

### Carry a window to every desktop

Press `⌃⌘G`. The window stays on its desktop and a bar appears at the top right of your other desktops. Rest on the bar to glance; click the picture or double-click the bar to return to the window. Press the shortcut again, or click × on the bar, to stop carrying it. Hidden or minimized windows show a snapshot labelled as not live.

### Title-bar gestures

With the pointer on a title bar, swipe up to roll up or restore a filled window, down to fill the screen, and sideways for a half-screen layout. Spread two fingers to fill, pinch to undo the last gesture placement, or double-tap with two fingers to toggle between filled and the previous size. Pull down or double-tap on a rolled-up bar to unroll it.

The feedback panel shows what releasing will do; pull back to cancel. Three notches of an ordinary scroll wheel count as one gesture. Magic Mouse support is included but awaits testing on real hardware. Safari keeps sideways swipes on its tabs; scrolling inside pages and lists is left alone. When Swish is running, title-bar gestures are left to it. This feature is on by default in Settings → Roll up.

### Faster roll-up and safer transitions

Roll-up and its animation start from a fast snapshot, then switch to live frames. Same-machine median time to the first visible change without animation fell from 342ms to 147ms; warm animation cover time fell from 271ms to 199ms. Conditions and limits are in the repository's performance notes.

This release also prevents an accidental green-button hover immediately after roll-up, respects switching back to an app during a glance, restarts live pictures when reentering a closing glance, and restores minimized carried windows before bringing them forward. Both READMEs and the bilingual website describe the new features.

## 安装与已知边界 / Installation and limits

下载 **WindowShade-v1.0.15.zip**，解压后移入“应用程序”。需要 **macOS 14+、Apple Silicon**，不含 Intel 二进制。附带 SHA-256 校验文件。

沿用现有 Apple Development 签名与应用标识，**尚未公证**。首次启动被阻止时，可在系统“隐私与安全性”中选择“仍要打开”。窗口操作需要辅助功能权限，实时画面需要屏幕录制权限。

Claude 阶段已在真触控板上验证滑动、张合、轻点两下及 Safari 标签共存。Magic Mouse、后台触感反馈、携带条在全屏 App 桌面上的显示仍未实机验证。9 月 26 日加入的三分之一、拐角、键盘排布、卷帘条上的快捷键和甩一下的松手判定，都在这台 Mac 上用真机探针跑通；鼠标真的甩一下、用力按卷帘条、插拔显示器还没逐项实机验证。快速截图使用已弃用的系统接口；不可用时退回 ScreenCaptureKit。最小化的窗口无法提供实时画面。具体设计与探针记录见 [看一眼](https://github.com/surfine/WindowShade/blob/v1.0.15/docs/glance.md)、[标题栏手势](https://github.com/surfine/WindowShade/blob/v1.0.15/docs/gestures.md)。

Download the ZIP, unzip it and move the app to Applications. Requires **macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon**; no Intel binary is included. A SHA-256 checksum is attached. The app keeps its Apple Development signature and is **not notarized**. If macOS blocks its first launch, use Open Anyway in Privacy & Security. Window actions require Accessibility; live pictures require Screen Recording.

Trackpad gestures and Safari tab coexistence were tested during development. Magic Mouse, background haptics and carried bars on full-screen app desktops remain unverified on real hardware. The September 26 additions (thirds, corners, keyboard placement, bar shortcuts and the flick's release check) pass the on-device probe; a real mouse flick, pressing firmly on a bar and plugging in a display have not each been checked by hand. Fast capture falls back to ScreenCaptureKit if the deprecated system capture API is unavailable. Minimized windows can only show snapshots.
