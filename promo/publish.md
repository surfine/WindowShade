**简介**

双击标题栏，窗口收成一条卷帘条，留在原处；再双击，原样回来。1994 年的 Mac 就能这样做，Mac OS X 之后没有了。WindowShade 把它做了回来，还多做了几件事：

- 指针停在卷帘条上，不用展开就能看一眼窗口里的内容
- 标题栏手势：往上推收起，往下拉铺满，左右滑占半屏；窗口跟着手指走，松手才算数
- 置顶、带到每张桌面、从 Dock 图标找到应用的每扇窗口

免费开源，macOS 14 及以上，Apple Silicon。
官网：https://windowshade.aaronlau.me
下载：https://github.com/surfine/WindowShade/releases/latest

**配套动态**

做了一支 44 秒的短片，讲 WindowShade 在做什么。

窗口一多，多数人会关掉或者最小化。其实还有第三种：双击标题栏，让窗口收成一条，留在原处。1994 年的 Mac 就能这么做，2001 年以后没了。

这一版加了标题栏手势：往上推收起，往下拉铺满，窗口跟着手指走，往回拉就当没发生过。

免费开源，欢迎来试，也欢迎来挑毛病。

**置顶评论**

几个常被问到的问题：
1. Intel Mac 能用吗？不能，只有 Apple Silicon 版，需要 macOS 14 以上。
2. 要什么权限？辅助功能用来找到和移动窗口；屏幕录制用来显示“看一眼”的画面，画面不离开你的电脑。
3. 装了 Swish 会冲突吗？不会。Swish 在运行时，标题栏手势交给它。
4. 下载地址在简介里。第一次打不开，看简介最后一段。

## YouTube

**横版视频标题**

The Mac lost this feature in 2001. I brought it back. | WindowShade

**说明**

Double-click a title bar and the window rolls up into a thin bar, right where it was. Double-click again and it's back. Macs could do this in 1994; Mac OS X dropped it in 2001.

WindowShade brings it back, plus a few things:
- Rest the pointer on a rolled-up bar to glance at the window without opening it
- Title-bar gestures: push up to roll up, pull down to fill the screen, swipe sideways for half the screen. The window follows your fingers and nothing happens until you let go.
- Pin a window, carry it to every desktop, find any window from its Dock icon

Free and open source. macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon.
Website: https://windowshade.aaronlau.me/en/
Download: https://github.com/surfine/WindowShade/releases/latest

The app isn't notarized yet. If macOS blocks the first launch, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

No screen recordings: everything in the film is animation, drawn to the app's real measurements. The music and sound effects were generated in code too.
